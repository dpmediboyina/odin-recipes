Biryani


Description

Biryani is a flavorful rice dish layered with spiced chicken, fragrant basmati rice, fried onions, and fresh herbs. It's slow-cooked so all the flavors mix together.

It's a classic for celebrations and weekend family meals in India.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500gm chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • Fresh mint and coriander
  • Salt to taste

Steps to cook

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, masala, ginger garlic paste, and salt for 1 hour.
  3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the marinated chicken until almost done.
  5. Boil the rice until it's about 70% cooked, then drain.
  6. Layer the rice over the chicken, top with fried onions, mint, and coriander.
  7. Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
