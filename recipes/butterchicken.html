Butter chicken

Description

Butter Chicken is a rich and creamy North Indian curry made with tender chicken cooked in a smooth tomato, butter, and cream sauce. It's mildly spiced, a little sweet, and super comforting.

It's one of the most popular Indian dishes around the world and goes perfectly with naan or steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger garlic paste, chili powder, garam masala, and salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Cook the marinated chicken in a pan until lightly charred, then set it aside.
  3. Melt the butter in the same pan and cook the onion until soft.
  4. Add the tomato puree and cook for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  5. Stir in the cream, sugar, and crushed fenugreek leaves.
  6. Add the chicken back into the sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with naan or rice.
