Kaju burfey

Description

Kaju Burfi is a classic Indian sweet made from cashews, sugar, and a touch of cardamom. It has a smooth, fudge-like texture that melts in your mouth, and it's usually cut into diamond shapes and sometimes topped with edible silver leaf.

It's a favorite during festivals like Diwali and is often shared as a gift at weddings and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cashews
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tsp ghee
  • Edible silver leaf (optional)

Steps

  1. Grind the cashews into a fine powder, pulsing in short bursts so they don't turn oily.
  2. Heat the sugar and water in a pan until the syrup reaches a one-string consistency.
  3. Lower the heat and add the cashew powder, stirring constantly to avoid lumps.
  4. Add the cardamom powder and ghee, and keep stirring until the mixture forms a soft dough that leaves the sides of the pan.
  5. Let it cool slightly, then knead it into a smooth dough.
  6. Roll it out between two sheets of parchment paper to about 1/4 inch thick.
  7. Add the silver leaf on top if using, then cut into diamond shapes and let it set.
